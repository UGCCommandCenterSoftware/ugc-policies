PRIVACY POLICY + TERMS PAGES: HOW TO PUT THEM ON GITHUB
========================================================
Upload ONLY the 4 files inside the "ugc-policies" folder: index.html, privacy.html, terms.html, logo.png.
Do NOT upload this README, and never upload your app code, database, credentials.json or any tokens.

STEPS
1. Finish your GitHub account (verify your email).
2. Click the + at the top right > New repository.
   Name:  ugc-policies   (any name works, but keep it simple and without spaces)
   Choose PUBLIC. (A free GitHub Pages site needs a public repository.) Click Create repository.
3. On the new repository page click "uploading an existing file" (or Add file > Upload files).
   Drag in the 4 files. Click "Commit changes".
4. Click Settings > Pages (left menu).
   Source: "Deploy from a branch".  Branch: main  and folder: / (root).  Click Save.
   Wait 1 to 3 minutes. Refresh the Pages screen until it shows your site address.
5. Your pages will be at (replace YOURUSERNAME with your GitHub name):
      https://YOURUSERNAME.github.io/ugc-policies/privacy.html
      https://YOURUSERNAME.github.io/ugc-policies/terms.html
      https://YOURUSERNAME.github.io/ugc-policies/index.html
   Open all three in your browser AND on your phone to check them.
6. In Partner Center:
   - Privacy policy URL  = the privacy.html address above.
   - Additional license terms box = paste the text of the Terms page (or the key sections), and mention the terms.html address.
   - Support contact email = support@ugccommandcentersoftware.com
7. Redirect from your own domain: set it up AFTER Microsoft approves the app, because the Store page
   address does not exist until then. You can also point a page on ugccommandcentersoftware.com to these pages.

KEEP IN MIND
 - Do not delete the repository later. If the pages disappear, your Store listing can be pulled.
 - If you change the app, update the pages and the "Effective" date near the top of each page.

BEFORE YOU PUBLISH: CHECK THESE STATEMENTS ARE TRUE FOR YOUR APP
 1. "The software does not send your information to us." This is based on the files I reviewed (the app, database,
    notifications, video folder, opener and clipboard code). If any other bundled file sends data to you (for example
    analytics or a link-check report), tell me and I will change the policy.
 2. Terms section 8 says "lifetime updates for as long as we continue to offer the Software". I added the last part
    to protect you, because "lifetime" has no end date. Change it if you prefer.
 3. Terms section 10 adds that Microsoft's refund policy applies to Store purchases, and that nothing limits your legal rights.
    Your manual's refund wording is repeated word for word before it.
 4. I am not a lawyer. Have a lawyer review both pages if you can. A governing-law clause (which state's law applies) is
    not included. A lawyer can add one.
